Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Forge Performance (the “App”) is a private player-development tool operated by Forge Academy (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The App is intended only for the Academy’s authorized coaches, administrators, players, and their families.

Information we handle

  • Account information — your email address (and display name) used to create your account and sign you in.
  • Player development data — evaluations, benchmark results, RISE UP values, coach feedback, and references to training and analysis videos. This is entered by the Academy’s coaches and administrators.
  • Player-authored entries — self-evaluations and self-feedback that a player chooses to record.

The App does not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment information, and it contains no third-party advertising or tracking.

How we use it

Information is used solely to provide the player-development dashboard — to display a player’s progress to that player, their family, and the Academy’s authorized staff. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The App stores data using Google services acting as our processors:

  • Google Firebase — authentication (sign-in) and the app database (Firestore).
  • Google Sheets / Google Apps Script — the Academy’s player records.

Supporting services include EmailJS (to send account-setup and password-reset emails), YouTube (training/analysis videos are linked or embedded from YouTube), and Google Fonts. Your use of linked YouTube content is also subject to Google’s privacy policy.

Access & security

Access requires a login, and data is restricted by role — coaches and administrators manage records, while a player sees their own information. We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children & families

The App is used within a youth sports academy and may contain information about players under the age of 18. That information is provided and managed by the Academy under its arrangement with each player’s family. Parents or guardians may request to review, correct, or delete their child’s information at any time using the contact below.

Data retention & deletion

Information is retained while a player is active with the Academy. You (or a parent/guardian) may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
